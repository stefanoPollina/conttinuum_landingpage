Continuum · Instruments for playing together

It began with a rhythm.

Continuum began at drum circles, jams and devotional gatherings, where hand percussion, voices and guitars play together. It started as a wish to bring new directions to that music, adding to it rather than leading it. Today it is a family of instruments you can play in your browser, on their own or alongside your own setup through MIDI and audio.

Mosaic Subtractive / FM

Carve with filters. Collide with frequencies.

Subtractive · FM · Wavetable

From seismic sub-bass and squelching acid to glassy FM bells and slow-moving soundscapes, Mosaic refuses to choose between warmth and wildness. Two layers of three oscillators combine subtractive filtering, FM, phase distortion and wavefolding, while sixteen sequenced LFOs and a deep wavetable engine keep every sound moving. Sculpt it, sequence it, morph it and capture the moment.

Listen · recorded on Mosaic
Launch instrument ↗
Explore the instrument · Full details

There have always been two ways to build a synth sound. Subtractive starts rich and carves it away: oscillators into filters, warmth into shape. FM starts pure and makes it collide: frequencies modulating frequencies until glass, metal and growl emerge.

Mosaic refuses to choose. Each layer pairs three oscillators and a deep multimode filter with FM, phase distortion or both at once. Stack the layers so the warm and the wild play together—a filtered bass beneath a glassy FM bell—or sweep a single patch from velvet to razor with a macro. Sixteen sequenced LFOs set it all moving, while the recorder catches the performance.

Subtractive × FM, in every layer

Two layers · up to 20 AudioWorklet voices. Each layer has three oscillators (saw, square, triangle, sine, white and pink noise) and wavefolding on every oscillator. OSC 1 provides four-voice unison, X-MOD and sub; OSC 2 and 3 offer sync and ring. Combine FM and phase distortion per layer, with envelopes, glide, velocity, key tracking and pitch bend.

Filters that do weather

Six filter modes per layer: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, comb and formant/vowel, with drive and a 12–24 dB blendable slope. The DRIP droplet/rain resonator adds EVEN, RAIN and BOTH patterns with ping, sweep, fall, bubble and wobble controls.

Sixteen LFOs, cross-wired

Eight per layer, each with LINK A+B. SIN, TRI, SQR, SAW and S&H waves with rate, morph, fold and retrigger. Route each LFO to oscillators, filters, FM/PD, DRIP and performance parameters; destinations can target layer A, B or both.

Modulation with a pulse

A programmable sequencer inside each LFO: up to 64 steps, with wave, step, wave×step, random and off modes, plus probability, ratchets, ties, slew and 18 starting patterns. The LFO SEQ umbrella orchestrates the 16 LFOs across up to 64 steps with crossfade, retrigger and 10 starting patterns.

Hands on the whole thing

Eight-slot modulation matrix with LFO, DRIP DROP/RAIN, envelope, velocity, mod wheel, aftertouch and key sources. Four macros per preset, an XY morph pad, four snapshots, randomise and mutate, and additional bank-layer modulation and chaos.

WAVE: a wavetable universe

131 tables in 16 banks, from foundation shapes and FM and formant tables to tribal, acid, psy and atmospheric pad banks. Scan and macros M1–M4 shape tables; the TABLE engine adds its own filter, envelope and unison, and can blend with or replace the main layers. Waveform, oscilloscope and spectrum displays, plus WAV table export.

MOTION

Eight tempo-synchronised step lanes with scene windows for evolving movement over bars.

The final polish

A full FX bus with drive, phaser, tempo delay, multiband echo, reverb with tone control, shimmer, compressor, DJ filter, chorus, ping-pong delay, crush, tremolo/autopan and stereo width, ending in a safety limiter. Final bus scenes and presets on FX cards.

Sequence it. Automate it. Play it.

A note-step sequencer with swing, gates, per-step probability and Euclidean patterns, plus an arpeggiator. AUTO/ARM automation can capture LFO and FX movements. Web MIDI input supports external performance controllers.

Capture everything

A performance recorder with overdub and WAV, OGG/Opus and MIDI export. Hundreds of factory presets, from bass, keys and pads to percussion, soundscapes and sequenced performances, with your own working prototypes kept at the top of the browser. Share sounds as .hlx and .hlxbank files.

Tip: presets that carry a sequence wait for you. Press play to hear them.

Continuum Rack

Eight banks. Eight engines each. One rack to rebuild.

Hybrid synthesis · Patchable rack

Make dub techno in your browser, then take it somewhere unexpected. Load one of 48 fully patched scenes, from acid house and ragga jungle to dub techno, techstep, tribal grooves and a run of acid rollers patched by hand in the rack itself. Pull it apart, reroute the cables and rebuild it with eight synthesis types, an eight-lane sequencer and hands-on sound shaping.

8⁶⁴ ways to build the rack

Eight banks of eight slots, and every slot can hold any of eight synthesis types. That makes 8⁶⁴, about 6.3 × 10⁵⁷ different racks: tens of millions of times more than there are atoms in the Earth. Not presets, but starting shapes, each one a different instrument to play.

Explore the instrument · Full details

CONTINUUM RACK is a patchable, eight-bank synthesis environment built for complete musical explorations—not just isolated sounds. Start with a fully configured genre scene, follow the signal flow, and make it your own.

  • 48 fully patched scenes: musical starting points spanning acid house, ragga jungle, dub techno, tribal and world influences, techstep, deep dub and acid rollers; each scene can be unpacked and remixed
  • Eight engine types in every slot: acid, FM, wavefold, wavetable, resonator, additive, cross-mod and hybrid architectures, each with its own controls, across eight banks of eight
  • Engine Atlas: 76 ready-made engine patches, including a set built on Mosaic’s wavetables, which load straight into the spectral wavetable engine
  • Patch cables: drag outputs into later banks and route LFO modulation, with visible cables spanning the rack rails
  • Acid character: expressive squelch with extensive resonance and filter-envelope shaping
  • Eight-lane sequencer: up to 64 steps per lane, with individual pitch, velocity, gate and filter settings, arpeggios and polymeters
  • Save, share and remix: export a complete rack or individual engine patches as files
  • Live performance: Web MIDI input and computer-keyboard playing
  • For the maths-minded: auditioning one rack layout every second, hearing all 8⁶⁴ would take about 2 × 10⁵⁰ years. The universe is around 1.4 × 10¹⁰ years old.

Sabre

Find the unexpected. Build something extraordinary.

Sampling · Granular · Live performance

Drop in a break or a whole track and SABRE slices it, evenly or by detecting the hits, then hands the slices to a piano roll where you can rebuild the groove from scratch. Alongside, the granular engine takes the same audio apart at a far finer level, turning a snare into a shimmer or a vocal into a drone, with every grain given its own pitch, direction, stereo position and resonant filter.

Then you stack it. Original breaks, rearranged chops and granular voices run across sixteen tracks, all locked to one master clock, each with its own filter, gate and echo. Ride the dials, record the moves, print the best phrases into loops and export the result. SABRE is a sampler, a granular synth and a performance instrument, all running on one clock.

Features

The sample deck

Slice · Detect · Rearrange
Load a one-shot, a break or a full song in WAV, AIFF, MP3, OGG, FLAC or M4A, and cut it into 4, 8, 16 or 32 slices, evenly or with adjustable hit detection. Play it whole or remap the slices, then sequence them on a piano roll of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars.

Sixteen tracks, one clock

Track Chopper · 16-track mix
Each lane holds its own break with its own edits, piano roll and full SABRE processing, all playing on the shared master transport. Sessions save everything, your audio included.

The granular engine

Grain Composer · Regions · Clouds
Drag across the waveform to define regions, assign them to pattern steps, or let them drift as a cloud. Shape grain size, density, position spray, scan speed, continuity and humanise. Granular presets set the grain, gate, filter and echo; your sample decides the timbre.

Every grain, individually shaped

Per-grain pitch, direction, space and filter
Each grain gets its own smooth window, pitch and pitch scatter, reverse chance, stereo spread, and a resonant low-pass filter with filter scatter. It’s movement at the smallest possible scale.

Effects that become material

Pre-effects · Bake into the grains
Run the source through drive, a low-pass filter and feedback echo before granulation, and the echoes become material the grains can read and reshape. Your original audio and slice markers stay untouched.

Performance layers

Performance Stable · Linked Performance
Load a performance recipe and SABRE builds the layers for you: a foundation break, skanking chops, bass and melodic grains, and a printed phase loop. Each performance layer has independent filter, gate and echo, and linked presets move all three together.

Gates with rhythm

From swell to sidechain to Euclid
Gate patterns include incremental opening and closing, swell, tempo pump, breakbeat ratchets, Euclidean rhythms, drum-grid pulses, and an external-sample sidechain where another sound keys the gate without being heard.

The extra rack

Twelve effects per track
Filter, gate, Beat Repeat, distortion/saturation, bit crusher, auto-pan, tremolo, ring modulator, phaser/flanger, tape delay, Dub Conversation and reverb, each independent per track. Beat Repeat captures 4, 8, 16 or 32 divisions live while the original keeps running, then releases you back exactly where you were.

Move it, then keep it

Draw · Record · Macros
Draw automation by hand, or record your dial and macro moves as you play. Swing and macro controls keep the groove loose and human.

The final mix

Mix bus · EQ · Sidechain
Choose which tracks duck under which, shape everything with a shared bus EQ, and finish through an output limiter. All of it is included in exports and saved sessions.

Print, export, share

Loops · WAV · Grooves
Print layered phrases into new loops, export processed WAV audio with optional effect tails, and save groove patterns and preset banks to share.

Closing

Bring a break. Leave with a world.

Load a sample · Your audio stays yours. Nothing plays until you press play.

Your source audio is kept separate from saved patterns and presets.

Fluidic

Glassy harmonics. Bent echoes. Dripping filters.

Drip synthesis · Audio effects · VST3

FLUIDIC is a non-Euclidean hybrid synthesizer built from drops. Play a note and it doesn’t just sound, it rains. Each drop is a tuned resonance that pings, sweeps, bubbles and wobbles as it lands. Patterns scatter the drops as gentle rain, splashes, streams, storms or a full 3D cavern. A tone layer, a dripping filter and a space of bent echoes and deep reverb shape it from glassy sine meditations to plinking dub bass. Then roll the dice: hold what you love and let chance rewrite the rest.

Play Fluidic ↗
Explore the instrument · Full details

FLUIDIC — drip synthesis

FLUIDIC is a drip synthesizer: every note falls as resonant drops of sound that ring, bend and scatter across the stereo field.

FLUIDIC FX — make your audio drip

FLUIDIC FX makes your audio drip. Feed it a breakbeat, a loop or a live input and the drops ring with your sound, locked to your DAW’s tempo. Pick a drip pattern and it reshapes the rhythm. Switch on auto-roll and the dice fire on the grid, straight or syncopated, so the effect keeps rewriting itself as the track plays.

Features

  • Drip synthesis: tuned resonant drops with ping, sweep, fall, bubble and wobble.
  • Seven drip patterns: even, rain, both, 3D, splash, stream and storm.
  • Glassy tone layer: sine, saw, square or noise.
  • Dripping filter: with envelope, LFO and key tracking.
  • Space: ping-pong echoes and generated reverb.
  • Dice: d4 to d20, a cursed d20 with no safe ranges, or roll the whole set. Hold any panel, row or single dial and let chance move the rest.
  • Auto-roll (FX): fires on the beat grid with straight, syncopated or custom step patterns.
  • Live visuals: see the drops from overhead or from the side.
  • Formats: in the browser, or as a VST3 instrument, a VST3 audio effect and a standalone app for Windows.
  • Your sounds: save, export and import your own bank.

Native development

Fluidic FX has migrated to a working native VST3 audio-effects plugin. Development and refinement continue as the native versions grow toward the full intended experience.

Behind the instruments

Made by ear.
Shaped by playing.

Every instrument here starts from a musical question: how should this bass move, what makes a groove feel human, how might one instrument answer another? Each is shaped by playing it. The voices, presets and scenes are chosen, tuned and arranged by ear, and features stay or go by how they feel under the hands.

They began as tools for personal creative use and are shared openly, so others can find their own pathways. Some are mature, some are young and moving fast, and each one shows its status plainly. It is an open invitation to experiment, perform and make something together.
