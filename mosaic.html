What's inside

Two ways to build a sound, played at once.

Subtractive starts rich and carves it away. FM starts pure and makes it collide. Each Mosaic layer pairs three oscillators and a deep multimode filter with FM, phase distortion or both, so a filtered bass can sit beneath a glassy bell in a single patch.

Voices

Subtractive × FM, in every layer

Two layers of three oscillators with wavefolding, unison, X-MOD, sync and ring. FM and phase distortion per layer, with envelopes, glide and velocity.

Filters

Filters that do weather

Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, comb and formant, with drive and a blendable slope. The DRIP resonator adds droplets and rain.

Movement

Sixteen sequenced LFOs

Eight per layer, each with its own step sequencer: probability, ratchets, ties and slew. LFO SEQ orchestrates all sixteen across 64 steps.

TABLE

A wavetable universe

131 tables in 16 banks, scanned and shaped with four macros. The TABLE engine blends with the main layers or replaces them, with MOTION step lanes.

FX bus

The final polish

Drive, phaser, tempo delay, multiband echo, reverb with shimmer, compressor, DJ filter, chorus, ping-pong, crush, tremolo and width.

Perform

Sequence it. Capture it.

A note sequencer with swing and Euclidean patterns, an arpeggiator, automation lanes, the BANK performance layer, and a recorder with WAV and MIDI export.

Mosaic for Windows

VST3 instrument + standalone app

Download installer Download zip
  • Mosaic-Windows-Setup.exeInstalls the VST3 and the standalone app
  • Mosaic-Windows-Plugins.zipThe same files, to copy by hand

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with the Microsoft WebView2 runtime (already part of Windows 11 and most up-to-date Windows 10 PCs). Works in any DAW that loads VST3 plugins on Windows, such as Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Bitwig Studio, Reaper and Studio One. Not for Pro Tools (AAX only) or Mac. All releases: GitHub.

This is an early build. The sound engine is checked against the browser Mosaic, but the plugin is still being tried in real sessions. If something misbehaves, the browser version is always there.

  1. Close your DAW, then run the installer. It puts Mosaic.vst3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, the standard folder every Windows DAW scans.
  2. Rescan your plugins in your DAW's plugin settings. For example, Ableton: Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan. FL Studio: Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins.
  3. Load Mosaic on an instrument track and play it from your keyboard or the piano roll. It follows your project's tempo, and its sequencer starts and stops with your transport.
  4. Save your project as usual: the sound you made is stored with it. Your own presets and banks are kept between sessions.
