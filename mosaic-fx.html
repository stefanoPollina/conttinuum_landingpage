Mosaic FX VST3 Plugin

The whole Mosaic FX bus, on any track.

Install
Version 0.1.0.58 · VST3 audio effect · Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · 5.0 MB
Download for WindowsSee the effects

The installer puts Mosaic FX.vst3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. It is a separate plugin from the Mosaic synth: installing or uninstalling one never touches the other. Colour, motion, space and master stages with their own FX presets, following your project’s tempo with no added latency. Works in any Windows DAW that loads VST3 plugins, such as Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Bitwig Studio, Reaper and Studio One; not Pro Tools (AAX only). Needs the Microsoft WebView2 runtime, already on Windows 11 and most up-to-date Windows 10 PCs. All releases: GitHub.

1 · Close your DAWThen run the installer.
2 · Rescan pluginsIn your DAW's plugin settings. Ableton: Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan. FL Studio: Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins.
3 · Put it on a trackAbleton: an audio track or return, under Audio Effects. FL Studio: a Mixer slot. Drums, vocals, a bass line or the whole mix.
4 · Save your projectEvery setting is stored with the project.

Early build: the effects are the same code as the Mosaic synth’s FX bus, and the plugin is still being tried in real sessions. Want to hear them on a synth first? Play Mosaic in your browser ↗
